Triax is the cable that links a broadcast camera to the truck, and the connectors on the ends take most of the abuse. Sooner or later one needs replacing, and it's a job that is much easier to approach after watching someone who has done it before.

This video walks through replacing triax connectors on broadcast camera cable. It's part of the *Truck Guy Training with Sunny D* material on the n392ep channel.

## Why I recommend it

I've worked with the person behind this channel and consider him a very good broadcast resource. When someone with real truck experience takes the time to show a repair on camera, it's worth watching, whether you're doing the job yourself or just want to understand what's involved.
